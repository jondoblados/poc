## AMBSAAS Annual Christmas Dinner 2026

Mark your calendars\! The Alliance Manchester Business School Alumni Association of Singapore (AMBSAAS) is hosting its Annual Christmas Dinner on Thursday, 3 December at 6:30 PM.\!

### Who It’s For

This festive gathering is designed for all Alliance Manchester Business School (AMBS) and University of Manchester alumni, former students, and friends in Singapore looking to close out the year together.

### Why You Should Attend

* **Reconnect & Expand Your Network:** Bridge cohorts and industries by catching up with familiar faces and making new professional connections across the Singapore Manchester community.
* **Celebrate the Season:** Unwind and enjoy an evening of festive dining, great conversation, and holiday cheer with fellow graduates.
* **Stay Connected to Manchester:** Tap into the collective knowledge, mentorship, and camaraderie that define the global Manchester network.
