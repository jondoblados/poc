#### Student Representative, Jan 2025 Cohort

Louis Chua is a Global MBA student at Alliance Manchester Business School and part of the AMBSAAS student representative team in Singapore. Professionally, he works in beauty education and brand strategy, with experience across regional training, consumer experience, and go-to-market execution in Asia Pacific.

He is passionate about connecting people, building meaningful learning communities, and creating practical opportunities for students and alumni to engage beyond the classroom. As a student representative, he aims to support a more active, inclusive, and collaborative AMBS community in Singapore, where members can share ideas, networks, and experiences.
