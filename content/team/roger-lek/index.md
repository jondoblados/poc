#### Emp@thy Lead, Council Member

Roger Lek is an MBA graduate specializing in enterprise IT transformations, cloud migrations, and AI platform governance. While his professional work revolves around guiding large-scale technology portfolios, his personal anchor is a commitment to presence. In a world that constantly encourages people to rush ahead, he chooses a different path—the path of presence. He values the clarity that comes from slowing down and finding inspiration in the simple act of observing daily life, believing that growth isn’t a race, but a continuous practice of learning who we are, one day at a time.
