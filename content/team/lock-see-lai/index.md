#### Honorary Secretary

Lock See brings a wealth of experience in ICT sales, consulting, operations, and transformation management to AMBSAAS. Currently, she leads the Telco Sales team at Dell Technologies, empowering communication service providers in Southeast Asia to transform their businesses and enhance service offerings. Her impressive career trajectory includes prior roles as the regional Telco Sales Leader for Southeast Asia & Korea at VMware Singapore and various international assignments with Ericsson across Europe and Africa.

Lock See has served two terms in AMBSAAS as the Honorary President and continues to support the association’s growth through its social events.
