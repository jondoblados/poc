#### Student Representative, Jan 2025 Cohort

Jon is a technology and engineering leader with over 25 years of experience building and running cloud, platform, and infrastructure for banks and high-growth tech companies. He has led multi-region platform engineering teams at Standard Chartered, driven some of the bank’s first containerisation efforts in Trade Finance, and helped deliver enterprise-scale cloud platforms while shaping capabilities in DevOps, SRE, automation, and observability. He takes a comprehensive end-to-end view of technology strategy, operating models, and regulatory alignment, ensuring the platforms he works on remain secure, resilient, and cost-efficient as they scale.

Outside of work, Jon is usually tinkering with new tech and writing about it, or on the court playing recreational tennis (4.2+ NTRP) or pickleball (2.75 DUPR). Growing up in the 80s hard-wired him with what he considers the best music and fashion of the era, which probably explains why he plays guitar and enjoys way too much karaoke.
