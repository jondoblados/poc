### AMBSAAS membership is exclusively open to

current students and graduates of AMBS only.

## Your Membership Benefits

![](image1.png)

#### Job Referrals

80% of jobs are in a hidden job market i.e. not advertised. With many of our AMBS alumni in positions of leadership and hiring, registered members can leverage the AMBSAAS network and get direct access to new career opportunities.

![](image2.png)

#### Coursework Advisory

It might be an unfamiliar core module that you need guidance on, or you might want to hear first-hand experiences before deciding on which 3 of the 22 electives for your tailored MBA experience.

![](image3.png)

#### Entrepreneurial Mentorship

Do you have an idea that simply will not let go? If so, you are in good company. At AMBSAAS, we have a thriving community of entrepreneurs. Women and men with ambitions and visions to build widgets, or simply provide services that will make a difference to their chosen groups of customers and audiences.

Be part of an entrepreneurial eco-system amongst AMBSAAS alumni via STE@M (Start-ups, Tech & Entrepreneurship @ Manchester) and [ACE (Action Community for Entrepreneurship)](https://ace.org.sg/).

![](image4.png)

#### Career & Personal Development

It is advantageous to be a protean careerist who never stops learning. Stay connected as we intersperse topics around industry knowledge and Masterclasses in our monthly events.

![](image5.png)

#### Financial Markets & China Interest Groups

Financial Markets & China Interest Groups are 2 specially-curated Telegram interest groups to gather like-minded individuals to discuss key economical and financial topics, improving literacy and awareness. [Contact secretariat](https://jon.doblados.net/poc/contact-us/) for participation.

![](image6.png)
